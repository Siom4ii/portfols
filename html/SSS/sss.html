SOCIAL SECURITY SYSTEM (SSS)
EDUCATIONAL ASSISTANCE LOAN PROGRAM (EALP)

    The Social Security System (SSS) Educational Assistance Loan Program (EALP)

    Is a financial support initiative established in 2012 to assist SSS members and their dependents with educational expenses for college degrees or vocational courses. Jointly funded by the National Government and SSS, the program provides loans of up to P20,000 per term for degree programs and up to P10,000 for vocational/technical courses.

    Here are the requirements for the SSS Educational Assistance Loan Program (EALP):

    • Must be an SSS member under 60 years old
    • Monthly income should not exceed P25,000.00
    • Must have at least 36 posted SSS contributions
    • At least six SSS contributions must be posted   within the last 12 months before applying
    • SSS loan payments must be up to date

Documents required:

• Accomplished EALP Application Form
• Valid ID or other valid documents
• School's assessment/billing statement
• Proof of monthly salary/income
• Proof of relationship to the beneficiary (if applicable)

Benefits:

For degree courses, members can borrow up to P160,000 for four-year programs and P200,000 for five-year programs. For vocational/technical courses, members can borrow up to P40,000 for two-year courses.
